About Rise From Broken

Rise From Broken, also known as RFB, is a smart multilingual search engine designed to help people discover information across the web.

What is Rise From Broken?

Rise From Broken provides a simple search experience for websites, images, videos, news and AI-powered answers.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a fast, lightweight and useful search platform that makes finding information easier for everyone.

Features

  • Video search
  • News search
  • RFB Ask AI assistant
  • Multilingual search support
