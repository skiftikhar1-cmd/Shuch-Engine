Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Rise From Broken ("RFB"). Rise From Broken is a multilingual search engine that provides web search, image search, video search, news search and AI-powered answers through RFB Ask.

We respect your privacy and aim to provide a simple and transparent search experience.

2. No Account Required

Rise From Broken does not require users to create an account or log in to use the search features.

We do not ask users to provide a name, password or personal profile in order to perform a normal search.

3. Search Queries

When you search using Rise From Broken, your search query may be sent to services used to provide search results and related features.

Depending on the feature you use, a search query may be sent to services such as Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, YouTube, Google News or other search providers used by the website.

These third-party services may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. RFB Ask

RFB Ask is the AI-powered assistant of Rise From Broken. When you submit a question to RFB Ask, the question is sent to our server-side API to generate an answer.

RFB Ask uses third-party services including Tavily for web search and Ollama Cloud for AI processing.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Supabase Search Database

Rise From Broken uses Supabase to provide part of its search functionality.

The search database contains search-related information such as titles, URLs, descriptions, keywords and related technical database fields.

The search database is not intended to be a user profile database.

6. Voice Search

Rise From Broken provides an optional voice-search feature using browser-supported speech recognition technology.

Voice input is processed through the browser's available speech-recognition functionality. The behavior of this feature may depend on your browser and its speech-recognition provider.

7. Local Storage

Rise From Broken may use your browser's local storage to remember your preferred dark or light theme.

This preference is stored locally in your browser and is used to improve the user experience.

8. Third-Party Websites and Services

Search results may contain links to third-party websites. These websites are not controlled by Rise From Broken.

Once you leave Rise From Broken and visit an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Rise From Broken does not control how third-party websites collect, use or store information.

9. Children's Privacy

Rise From Broken is designed to provide a simple and accessible search experience for general users.

We do not require users to create accounts or provide personal information to perform a normal search.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of internet search services and external websites, as search results may lead to third-party content that Rise From Broken does not control.

10. Security

We aim to keep Rise From Broken's services reasonably secure and to avoid unnecessarily collecting personal information.

However, no internet service can guarantee complete security of information transmitted over the internet.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Rise From Broken, its features or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
